# ONYX V3 Product & Brand Specification

## Version

**ONYX V3 — Working Family Capital**

Public brand: **ONYX Wealth & Wellness**. “Club” is not part of current public-facing brand language.

## Audience and Positioning

ONYX Wealth & Wellness is an education-first financial planning and decision-guidance platform for W-2 professionals, 1099 earners, and modern growing families. Typical users are in family- and career-building years; no age or income figure is a hard eligibility threshold.

The platform helps people identify overlooked financial questions and build coordinated solutions around Payroll & Tax, Retirement Planning, Children’s Education Funding, and Family Protection. It is not positioned as a high-net-worth-only service.

## Brand Promise and Method

> The most expensive financial question may be the one you didn't know to ask.

The method is **Discover → Understand → Decide**. Customer outcomes are **Keep More → Build for Tomorrow → Fund Their Future → Protect the Plan**.

## Four Primary Knowledge Paths

1. **Keep More — Payroll & Tax:** organize W-2, withholding, 1099, side-business, expense, estimated-tax, entity/payroll, employer-benefit, HSA, and tax-organization questions.
2. **Build for Tomorrow — Retirement Planning:** understand retirement accounts, self-employed plans, surplus capital, future income, real estate, business value, and work optionality as one structure.
3. **Fund Their Future — Children’s Education Funding:** examine time, compounding, 529 and aid awareness, ownership, flexibility, and education-versus-retirement trade-offs.
4. **Protect the Plan — Family Protection:** begin with liquidity, income interruption, family obligations, mortgage continuity, and business dependency; proceed from identified risk to education, alternatives, needs analysis, and licensed review.

The primary CTA is **Explore My Capital Map**, followed by **Something Changed**. **4-Minute Capital Check** is tertiary and remains an optional tool.

## CTA Rules

Use contextual review language after education and need recognition: “Review My Payroll & Tax Questions,” “Review My Retirement Structure,” “Review My Education Funding Plan,” “Review My Family Protection Gap,” and “Review Before I Act.” “Ask Sammi” remains a fallback.

## Knowledge Guide Standard

Every future guide should support:

1. What You Asked
2. What It Connects To
3. The Question You Didn’t Know to Ask
4. Why It Matters in Dollars
5. Your Choices & Trade-Offs
6. Before You Act
7. Your Next Move

Optional supporting content includes calculators, checklists, case studies, official sources, related knowledge, DIY boundaries, professional-review triggers, and a contextual service handoff. Year-specific tax content should support Tax Year, Last Reviewed, and Primary Source metadata.

## Education Disclaimer

Educational guidance, not individualized tax, legal, investment, or insurance advice. Tax rules and financial circumstances can change. Product recommendations, where applicable, require a separate needs analysis and appropriate licensed review.

## Wellness

Wellness is **the human capacity behind the financial plan**: energy and work capacity, healthy aging, family wellness, sustainable habits, and optional resources. It remains education-first, non-scoring, non-sensitive, and never assumes a USANA recommendation.

## Preserved V2 Platform

Capital Map, Foundation V2, V1 Assessment, Event Radar, Hero Journeys, Wellness nodes, Long-Term Map, worksheets, Next Dollar Planner, and Survey remain functional supporting tools beneath the V3 public architecture.

## Phase 2 — Hero Knowledge Guide Engine

Four bilingual Hero Guides establish the first reusable decision-guide layer:

- W-2 → Second Income Engine
- Small Business → S Corporation / Payroll / Retirement
- New Baby → Children’s Education Funding
- Rental Equity → Family Capital Repositioning

Each guide uses one centralized schema and one renderer. The seven Knowledge Guide concepts appear in one scrollable decision map with useful guidance near the top; deeper sections remain optional. Guides may include a story, flow map, topic reflection, trade-off comparison, calculator, information-gathering list, official source, related guide, printable summary, and contextual Sammi handoff.

Guide interactions are non-scoring and page-memory only. Result objects expose `guideId`, explored and connected topics, planning window, missing information, next moves, money lens, related guides, and Sammi review context for a future Capital Action Board. Raw answers and sensitive personal data are not stored.

The Money Lens vocabulary is internal and non-scoring: **KEEP, BUILD, FUND, PROTECT, OPTIONALITY**.

Phase 2 calculators are educational only:

- Business Profit Lens: revenue minus estimated legitimate business expenses; no personalized tax calculation.
- Cost of Waiting: illustrative future value of monthly contributions at different starting ages; returns are not guaranteed and taxes, fees, product costs, and financial-aid effects are excluded.
