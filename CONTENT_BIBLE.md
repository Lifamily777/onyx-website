# Mission

Help people make wiser decisions.

Every piece of ONYX content exists to make one part of a complex system easier to understand — in service of the voice and philosophy defined in [BRAND_BIBLE.md](./BRAND_BIBLE.md).

---

# Content Types

- **Insights** — short, evergreen educational articles (the platform's core content unit).
- **Guides** — longer, structured walkthroughs of a specific system or decision.
- **Videos** — spoken explanations of Insights or Guides, for a different learning style.
- **Newsletter** — periodic digest connecting recent Insights to a single theme.
- **Case Studies** — anonymized, real-world illustrations of a system in practice.

---

# Writing Principles

Explain complexity.

Avoid hype.

Use evidence.

Respect uncertainty.

Never promise certainty.

---

# Insight Structure

Every Insight follows the same shape, regardless of pillar or platform:

1. **Headline** — states the idea plainly.
2. **Opening Question** — the question a reader is already asking themselves.
3. **Context** — why this matters right now.
4. **Explanation** — the underlying system, explained plainly.
5. **Examples** — concrete, relatable illustrations.
6. **Decision Framework** — how a reader can reason about their own situation.
7. **Actionable Takeaway** — the one thing worth remembering or doing next.
8. **CTA** — an invitation to go deeper with ONYX, never a pressured sell.

This structure is editorial, not technical — see [ENGINEERING.md](./ENGINEERING.md) (Principle 8: Content Is Data) for how Insights are stored and rendered in the codebase.

---

# Editorial Standards

Prefer original thinking.

Quote sources.

Separate facts from opinions.

Never publish AI-generated claims without verification.

4. Review
