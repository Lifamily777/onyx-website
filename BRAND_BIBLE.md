# Mission

The world is becoming more complex.

Understanding it shouldn't be.

ONYX exists to help people understand the systems shaping their money, health, technology, and everyday life — so they can make clearer, more confident decisions.

---

# Core Philosophy

Technology should reduce entropy.

Human judgment always comes first.

ONYX does not exist to replace expertise, institutions, or professional advice. It exists to make the systems people already live within easier to understand — so effort and technology can be used more intentionally, not blindly.

---

# Three Pillars

## Wealth

Understand the systems behind money.

Taxes, insurance, retirement, estate planning, family finance, business structure, and risk — explained clearly enough to act on.

## Wellness

Understand the systems supporting health.

Nutrition, prevention, healthy routines, and the everyday choices that build long-term energy and resilience.

## Intelligence

Understand the systems shaping how we think and work.

AI, automation, and decision frameworks — always with human judgment kept at the center.

---

# Brand Personality

- **Clear** — says what it means, without jargon for its own sake.
- **Modern** — reflects how people actually learn and live today.
- **Calm** — never rushed, never alarmist.
- **Optimistic** — believes understanding leads somewhere better.
- **Intellectually honest** — states what is known, what is uncertain, and what is not yet answered.

---

# Design Philosophy

Minimal.

Elegant.

Timeless.

No unnecessary complexity.

Every visual choice should make a system easier to understand, not decorate it.

---

# Voice & Tone

Explain.

Never exaggerate.

Never manipulate.

Prefer clarity over persuasion.

ONYX speaks as a knowledgeable, patient guide — never as a salesperson, and never with false urgency.

---

# Decision Framework

When uncertain about a brand, product, or communication decision, ask:

1. Does this reduce complexity?
2. Does this preserve human judgment?
3. Does this strengthen long-term trust?

If not, don't do it.

For how this framework applies to engineering decisions, see [ENGINEERING.md](./ENGINEERING.md). For how it applies to published content, see [CONTENT_BIBLE.md](./CONTENT_BIBLE.md).
