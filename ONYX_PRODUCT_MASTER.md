# ONYX Product Master Blueprint

## 1. ONYX Life Capital Map

The ONYX Life Capital Map is the long-term product framework for helping people understand, organize, and improve the capital systems supporting their lives. It is not merely a score or lead form; it is an educational map connecting questions, explanations, tools, planning events, and appropriate next steps.

## 2. Wealth × Wellness

ONYX uses a two-dimensional architecture:

- **Vertical:** the six stages of life capital development
- **Horizontal:** Wealth and Wellness

The two domains remain distinct while sharing a common planning language and stage structure.

## 3. Six Capital Stages

1. Survival Capital
2. Stability Capital
3. Protection Capital
4. Growth Capital
5. Strategic Capital
6. Optionality & Legacy Capital

The stages form an ordered planning ladder, not a promise that life progresses in a perfectly linear way.

## 4. Current V1 Capital Assessment

The current Capital Assessment is a 30-question, bilingual, versioned assessment with centralized question data, deterministic scoring, capital-layer results, Next Dollar guidance, capital-structure dimensions, risk profile, temperament, legacy orientation, safe local progress storage, validation profiles, and automated tests.

V1 is educational. It must not be treated as individualized tax, legal, investment, insurance, or medical advice.

## 5. V2 Direction

V2 should combine three time horizons and a reusable learning system:

- a 15–20 question **Foundation**
- a 12–24 month **Event & Opportunity Radar**
- a 5–10 year **Long-Term Planning Map**
- an Insight Library
- educational calculators
- downloadable worksheets
- contextual Ask Sammi handoffs

The experience should reveal useful context progressively instead of forcing every user through one long questionnaire.

## 6. Trust-First Philosophy

ONYX earns trust by making complexity understandable, assumptions visible, and self-management possible where appropriate. Calls to action should follow education and need recognition. Some pages exist mainly to help users, not to sell.

## 7. Long-Term 72-Node Architecture

The long-term map targets 72 structured nodes across six stages and two domains. Nodes should be centralized data objects rendered through reusable components. Each meaningful node should support bilingual explanation, a real-life question or story, self-management guidance, deeper-review triggers, related events, tools or worksheets where appropriate, Insight connections, and contextual Ask Sammi information.

The 72-node target is a roadmap, not permission to publish placeholder pages.

## 9. First-Priority Events

- Sell a rental property
- Start a business
- Add a partner or investor
- Owner exit
- Buy a business
- Sell a business
- S Corporation election or owner compensation change
- Retirement approaching
- Significant gift or inheritance
- Move to another state

Event content should emphasize planning windows, records to gather, questions worth asking, relevant nodes, and cautious current-law language.

## 10. Wellness Long-Term Direction

Wellness should extend from Survival through Optionality & Legacy using the same educational stage model. Future work may integrate health knowledge, practical tools, and optional resources. Assessments must remain neutral and education-first; optional products or affiliations must not become predetermined conclusions.

## 11. Revenue Architecture

The intended sequence is:

> knowledge → tool → trust → planning need → consultation → appropriate service/product → ongoing relationship

Internal commercial relevance may help prioritize development, but user-facing language should describe needs and readiness rather than lead value.

## 12. Key Principle

Some pages exist mainly to help users, not to sell. Their value is trust, clarity, usefulness, and the user’s ability to make a better decision.
